**Profile:** 70kg, 5.8ft (BMI ~23.4, Healthy Weight Range). Focus is metabolic health, insulin sensitivity, and glucose disposal via skeletal muscle optimization.
**Dietary Rule:** Non-vegetarian and egg meals allowed Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Sunday. Strictly vegetarian (no meat, no eggs) on Monday and Saturday.

## Part 1: Phase-by-Phase Nutritional Framework

### Baseline Macronutrient Guide
- **Daily Caloric Target:** ~1,600 - 1,700 kcal (Maintenance/Slight deficit to preserve lean mass).
- **Protein Target:** 85g - 100g daily (~1.2 - 1.4g/kg) to maintain muscle mass for glucose disposal.
- **Carbohydrate Target:** 130g - 150g daily, focusing exclusively on complex, high-fiber, low-glycemic sources.
- **Fiber Target:** 30g+ daily to slow down glucose absorption in the gut.

### Weekly Meal Archetypes

#### Veg Archetype (Monday & Saturday)
*   **Breakfast:** Moong dal cheela (2) with spinach and paneer stuffing OR Oats porridge (unflavored oats) made with unsweetened almond milk/water, topped with 1 tbsp chia seeds and 10 almonds.
*   **Lunch:** 1 bowl of brown rice or 2 multigrain rotis + 1 big bowl of mixed dal or chickpeas (chole) + 1 large bowl of non-starchy sabzi (bhindi, broccoli, cauliflower, or cabbage) + 1 cup of curd (unsweetened).
*   **Evening Snack:** 1 cup of roasted chana (chickpeas) OR cucumber/carrot sticks with hung curd dip.
*   **Dinner:** Paneer bhurji (150g paneer) cooked with tomatoes and capsicum + 1 small multigrain roti + large green salad with olive oil dressing.

#### Non-Veg/Egg Archetype (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Sunday)
*   **Breakfast:** 3 egg-white scrambled eggs with mushrooms, onions, and spinach + 1 slice of whole-wheat toast (optional) OR 2 whole boiled eggs + 1 cup of sautéed sprouts.
*   **Lunch:** 150g grilled/tandoori chicken breast or grilled fish + 1 bowl of quinoa or brown rice + 1 large bowl of steamed broccoli, zucchini, and bell peppers.
*   **Evening Snack:** A handful of walnuts and almonds + 1 cup of green tea.
*   **Dinner:** Chicken or fish curry (cooked in minimal oil, light tomato-onion base) + 1 small multigrain roti or bowl of cauliflower rice + a large mixed green salad.

## Part 3: Golden Rules of Glucose Control for Your Profile
1. **The Food Order Trick:** Always eat fiber/vegetables first, then proteins and fats, and save complex carbohydrates (rice, roti, quinoa) for the very end of the meal. This slows gastric emptying and drastically blunts the post-meal glucose spike.
2. **Never Skip the Post-Meal Nudge:** A 10-15 minute walk within 30 minutes of finishing a meal forces muscles to absorb circulating glucose without requiring insulin.
3. **The 48-Hour Constraint:** Never let more than 48 hours pass without a structured workout. The insulin sensitivity boost gained from a Cult Home strength session drops significantly after 48 hours. Keep the cellular signal alive.
